Your Grace, The Duke of Westminster,\\

You may be aware that I signed a contract with your late father in 2010 and I return now with the state of my somewhat mature response to that brief in good faith, having fulfilled my promise.
Note, there is a significant element to the material I have personally witnessed and now share with you that many consider sacred, at least amongst the brothers of Abraham and probably beyond.
Therefore, a new foreign order appears appropriate, where there appears scarce guidance available.
It would not suprise me if you find the formal title I am using shocking, you have more experience here than I.
I may have made a mistake?
However, to me this appears the result of the new foreign inter-networking order of the Commonwealth of Peoples.\\

As the rightful Fleet Admiral, I have returned with a reasonable payload that has legitimacy to be recognised as Sovereign globally.
The society is technicaly gifted and foreign to all Nation States.
I recognise that your House has vast financial wealth and expertise in foreign diplomacy.
As this letter is essentialy a formal approach by a foreign mission, I hope you understand why I believe the Duchy of Westminster may have the right to first refusal and influence.
Therefore, I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to showcase the development of the Commonweath of Peoples with you, to consider investment opportunities and partnerships.
There may also be a serious case for a new foreign embassy/institution of faith.\\

The majority of our Intellectual Property is in holdings and not yet operational.
We have returned with the following response payload, organised into subsidiary vehicles: Colours of Averlorn; Fringe Papers; Gillett Instruments; Hoshi Property; Mettle Studio.\\

I enclose sample products from these viable businesses and would appreciate a thoughtfull audience within reasonable time.
I dearly wish for there to be no question of potential insult in our correspondence.\\

Yours truly,\\

His Grace, Prince Aidan the Arch Duke of Anirain GMCOP
