Your Grace, Hugh Grosvenor the Duke of Westminster,\\

You may be aware that I signed a contract with your late father in 2010 and I return now with the state of my somewhat mature response to that promise in good faith.
Note, there is a significant element to this new content I have personally witnessed and now share with you that many consider sacred, at least amongst the brothers of Abraham and probably beyond.
As I appear to be a Sovereign of new territory, and mindful not to step on others toes, I've been inspired by folklore for a distributed system of governance.
I would not be offended at all if you find my chosen consortium surprising, you certainly have more experience with these matters than I.
It just appears to me a reasonable outcome of my efforts.
Perhaps being more familiar with the traditions of titles and styles you may understand a better way?\\

I believe I am a rightful Arch Duke of a Commonwealth of Peoples with legitimacy by original constitution.
Many of this Commonwealths products utilise digital technology and are incredibly scalable, others more like works of art and not for mass production.
The majority of our Intellectual Property is in holdings and not yet operational.
We have returned with the following response payload, organised into subsidiary vehicles: Colours of Averlorn; Fringe Papers; Gillett Instruments; Hoshi Property; Mettle Studio.\\

Mettle Studio is the only subsidiary that has successfully operated for some time, as a firm of designers that specialise in rapid prototyping.
However, I see potential for something much more exciting.
Particularly, if I can organise the Commonwealth of Peoples Houses to work more effectively together.\\

The Commonwealth of Peoples is technically gifted and a foreign inter-networking consortium to all Nation States.
I recognise that your House has vast financial wealth and expertise in foreign diplomacy.
As this letter is essentially a formal approach by a foreign mission, I hope you understand why I believe the Duchy of Westminster may have the right to first refusal and influence over further development.
Therefore, I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to discuss the particulars of the Commonweath of Peoples with your House, to consider any potential investment opportunities and partnerships.
There may also be a serious case for a new foreign embassy/institution of faith, as an Official custodian of the original smart contracts involved.\\

I'f also like to propose a fundraising initiative to raise "The Endurance" for our community, a vehicle set for sustained strategic investment in core infrastructure.
The Endurance successfully spans the nexus between defence and civilian interests, supported by a lawful online community that plays to strengths.
Leaders are set against Arificial Intelligence, with a brief to better the software time, after time.
Our focus is on the successful deployment of resources and world class environmental management.\\

I would appreciate a thoughtful audience within reasonable time.
There seems much potential here in private equity, as well as Sovereign wealth funds.
However, this really seems the best we can do whilst operating on shoestring maintenance.
If anyone could seriously evaluate where we've arrived at, suggest necessary course corrections and consider reasonable investment, I'd be very happy.
Whilst I may always risk unfortunate embarrassment, I dearly wish for there to be no question of potential insult in our correspondence.\\

Yours truly and in my informal style (for which I am more confident),\\

Dr Aidan T Parkinson BEng(Hons) MSc Phd(Cantab) GMCOP CEng MCIBSE\\
