Your Grace, The Duke of Westminster,\\

You may be aware that I signed a contract with your late father in 2010 and I return now with the state of my somewhat mature response to that brief in good faith, having fulfilled my promise.
Note, there is a significant element to the material I have personally witnessed and now share with you that many consider sacred, at least amongst the brothers of Abraham and probably beyond.
Therefore, a new foreign order appears appropriate, where there appears scarce guidance to follow.
As I appear to be a Sovereign Duke of the inter-network, and mindful not to step on others toes, I've been inspired by virtual reality.
I would not be offended at all if you find the formal title I am using surprising, you certainly have more experience with these matters than I.
This appears the reasonable result of the Commonwealth of Peoples to me.
Though, you may understand a better way?\\

As the rightful Fleet Admiral of the inter-network, I have returned with a reasonable payload that has global legitimacy.
The Commonwealth of Peoples is technicaly gifted and foreign to all Nation States.
I recognise that your House has vast financial wealth and expertise in foreign diplomacy.
As this letter is essentialy a formal approach by a foreign mission, I hope you understand why I believe the Duchy of Westminster may have the right to first refusal and influence over further development.
Therefore, I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to discuss the particulars of the Commonweath of Peoples with your House, to consider investment opportunities and partnerships.
There may also be a serious case for a new foreign embassy/institution of faith.\\

The majority of our Intellectual Property is in holdings and not yet operational.
We have returned with the following response payload, organised into subsidiary vehicles: Colours of Averlorn; Fringe Papers; Gillett Instruments; Hoshi Property; Mettle Studio.\\
Mettle Studio is the only subsidiary that has successfully operated for some time, as a firm of designers that specialise in rapid prototyping.
However, I see potential for something much more exciting.\\

I'd also like to propose a charity fundraising event that you may consider partnering with.
The care of newborn babies, whether human or a pet, is a critical period that puts acute stresses on families.
Having struggled severely with my mental health during these times, I have witnessed the state of acute care for those who become dangerously unwell.
My personal experiences were typically disorientating and traumatic.
Such matters have been made worse by the clear lack of investment in NHS acute wards.
"The Well Come Trust" is a planned charity trek for our Peoples through the trails of Scotland in May 2026.
Four groups will set off on trails that match their own ambitions to raise funds for digital investment in psychological safety and maternity care.
For example: Caledor may attempt The Cape Wrath Trail, whilst adolescents could simply follow the West Highland Way.
Those who participate will be awarded Fellowships of the Mission Impossible Team, and may use FMIT post-nominals.

I would appreciate a thoughtfull audience within reasonable time.
There seems much potential, but this really seems the best we can do whilst operating on shoestring budgets.
If anyone could evaluate where we've arrived, suggest necessary course corrections and consider reasonable investment, I'd be very happy.
I dearly wish for there to be no question of potential insult in our correspondence.\\

Yours truly,\\

His Grace, Prince Aidan the Arch Duke of Anirain GMCOP
